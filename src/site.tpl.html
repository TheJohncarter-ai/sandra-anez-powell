About the artist

Sandra Añez Powell

Born in Venezuela, Sandra spent many years in Kentucky before making her home in Annapolis, Maryland, with her husband and two sons.

For a long time family, work and community were her whole life — until a friend asked about her hobbies and she realized she had none. She had always loved art, so she picked up a brush. Painting became her outlet from the stresses of the world, and then her language: color, figures, texture, feeling.

Today she paints the Chesapeake as a celebration — sails in colors no sailmaker ever stitched — alongside portraits, women drawn as monuments, flowers at the scale of a window, and abstracts where the paint simply plays.

“I transfer those feelings, images and emotions to a white canvas that is transformed into a unique and special pedacito de arte — a little piece of art. When I paint, I find joy.”

S. Añez Powell

Art in Action

Painting workshops that build connection, confidence and community

Sandra brings the paint, the canvases and the encouragement to your group. Nobody needs to have painted before — that is rather the point. En inglés o en español.

  • Teams at workTeam building that gets people talking, away from the meeting room.
  • Community groups & nonprofitsCreative sessions built around connection and wellbeing.
  • Women’s & Latino groupsBilingual sessions rooted in Sandra’s own community work.
  • Schools & after-schoolHands-on painting for students, at their own scale.
  • CelebrationsBirthdays, showers and reunions, with a painting to take home.

Creative, hands-on art activities are a proven way to build trust, boost emotional wellbeing and spark joy in a group — turning collaboration into a shared, memorable experience. Art-based sessions break down formal barriers, encourage natural conversation, and create a safe space for expression.
